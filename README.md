# RM Analytics Lab

## Revenue Management Analytics & Decision Science

An interactive analytical laboratory exploring the economics, forecasting, performance measurement, and commercial decision-making behind airline Revenue Management.

**Live Demo:**  
https://ararsahaile0078-dev.github.io/revenue-management-analytics-lab/

## Overview

Revenue Management (RM) is fundamentally a decision problem.

Airlines must continuously balance:

- Demand
- Capacity
- Pricing
- Forecasting
- Inventory
- Revenue
- Operating cost
- Profitability
- Customer behaviour
- Commercial strategy

The objective is not simply to sell more seats.

The objective is to make **better commercial decisions with limited capacity and uncertain demand**.

The RM Analytics Lab is designed as an interactive environment for exploring these relationships.

---

## What This Project Does

The laboratory connects Revenue Management concepts to practical analytical decision-making.

The project currently includes:

### 1. Revenue Management Problem

Introduces the commercial problem behind airline Revenue Management and explains why demand, capacity, forecasting, pricing, and profitability must be considered as an interconnected system.

### 2. Revenue Management Framework

Organises the main analytical components of Revenue Management into a decision framework.

The framework connects:

**Demand → Forecasting → Capacity → Pricing → Revenue → Cost → Profitability → Decision**

### 3. Forecasting Laboratory

An interactive forecasting environment for examining demand assumptions and forecasting behaviour.

The laboratory is designed to help explore:

- Demand levels
- Demand volatility
- Forecasting methods
- Forecast accuracy
- Capacity implications
- Commercial decision-making

### 4. KPI Intelligence

An interactive commercial performance dashboard connecting operational assumptions to Revenue Management KPIs.

The dashboard includes:

- Revenue
- Operating cost
- Operating profit
- Profit margin
- Load factor
- Revenue per passenger
- Yield
- RASK
- CASK
- Break-even load factor

The dashboard allows users to change commercial assumptions and observe how the KPI system responds.
