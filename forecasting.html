Forecasting as a commercial system

A forecast is not simply a number. It is an analytical estimate used to support capacity, pricing and inventory decisions under uncertainty.

Forecasting Models

Four ways to estimate demand.

Different forecasting methods respond differently to trend, volatility and changing booking behaviour.

01 — MOVING AVERAGE

Moving Average

Smooths recent observations and provides a simple baseline forecast for relatively stable demand.

02 — EXPONENTIAL

Exponential Smoothing

Gives greater importance to recent observations and reacts faster to changing demand conditions.

03 — TREND

Trend Adjusted

Extends recent demand direction to capture systematic growth or decline in the booking environment.

04 — SEASONAL

Seasonal Pattern

Uses a recurring pattern to represent systematic variation across the booking horizon.
