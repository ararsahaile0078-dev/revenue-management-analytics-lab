01 / Forecasting Methods

Four useful ways to estimate future demand.

Different forecasting methods can produce different views of future demand. The correct method depends on data quality, trend behaviour and commercial context.

MODEL 01

Moving Average

Uses the average of recent observations to smooth short term fluctuations and establish a stable baseline.

MODEL 02

Exponential Smoothing

Gives more importance to recent demand observations and responds faster when demand changes.

MODEL 03

Trend Forecast

Estimates a linear demand trend from historical observations and projects that trend forward.

MODEL 04

Growth Scenario

Applies a commercial growth assumption to the recent demand level to create a planning scenario.

04 / Booking Curves

Demand develops before departure.

Revenue Management teams monitor how bookings accumulate toward departure to understand whether demand is tracking above or below expectations.

Typical Booking Curve

  • 60+ days before departure 15%
  • 45 days before departure 30%
  • 30 days before departure 48%
  • 14 days before departure 68%
  • 7 days before departure 84%
  • Departure day 100%

Commercial Questions

  • Is demand ahead of forecast?
  • Are bookings accelerating?
  • Is remaining capacity sufficient?
  • Should price levels change?
  • Is the forecast bias increasing?
  • Should inventory protection increase?
