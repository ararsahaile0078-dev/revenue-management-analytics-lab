01 / Forecasting Methods

Four useful ways to estimate future demand.

Different forecasting methods can produce different views of future demand. The appropriate method depends on data quality, trend behaviour and commercial context.

MODEL 01

Moving Average

Uses the average of recent observations to smooth short term fluctuations and establish a stable demand baseline.

MODEL 02

Exponential Smoothing

Gives more importance to recent demand observations and provides a responsive estimate when demand changes.

MODEL 03

Linear Trend

Estimates a linear relationship from historical demand and projects that trend into future periods.

MODEL 04

Commercial Scenario

Forecasts can be interpreted as planning scenarios for capacity, pricing and inventory decisions.

04 / Booking Curves

Demand develops before departure.

Revenue Management teams monitor how bookings accumulate toward departure to understand whether demand is tracking above or below expectations.

Typical Booking Curve

  • 60+ days before departure 15%
  • 45 days before departure 30%
  • 30 days before departure 48%
  • 14 days before departure 68%
  • 7 days before departure 84%
  • Departure day 100%

Commercial Questions

  • Should price levels change?
