KPI REFERENCE

Know what the numbers mean.

These measures connect airline operations with Revenue Management and profitability.

Load Factor

Passengers ÷ Capacity

Measures how much available passenger capacity is being utilized.

RPK

Passengers × Distance

Revenue passenger kilometres measure passenger traffic carried.

ASK

Seats × Distance

Available seat kilometres measure the capacity offered to the market.

Yield

Passenger Revenue ÷ RPK

Measures revenue generated for each passenger kilometre.

RASK

Revenue ÷ ASK

Measures revenue generated from each available seat kilometre.

CASK

Operating Cost ÷ ASK

Measures operating cost per available seat kilometre.

Profit Margin

Profit ÷ Revenue

Shows how much of the generated revenue remains after operating costs.

Break-even Load Factor

Break-even passengers ÷ Capacity

Indicates the minimum utilization required to cover operating costs under the scenario assumptions.

Profit per Passenger

Profit ÷ Passengers

Shows the average economic contribution generated by each passenger.

Revenue

Fare Revenue + Ancillary Revenue

Represents the commercial income generated by the flight.

Operating Cost

Fixed + Variable Operating Costs

Represents the resources required to operate the flight under the scenario.

Operating Profit

Total Revenue − Operating Cost

Shows whether the flight produces a positive or negative operating contribution.
