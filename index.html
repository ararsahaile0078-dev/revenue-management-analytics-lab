01 — The Commercial Problem

Revenue Management begins with a constrained resource.

Airline capacity is limited, perishable and time-sensitive. The analyst therefore has to continuously balance uncertain demand, price, capacity, customer value and competitive pressure.

Revenue Management is not simply about filling seats.

It is about deciding which demand to accept, at what price, at what time, given limited and perishable capacity. The objective is to improve the economic value generated from constrained inventory.

02 — Analytical Framework

From signal to decision.

A strong Revenue Management analyst does more than calculate KPIs. The analyst connects measurement, explanation, forecasting and optimization before making a commercial decision.

01 — MEASURE

Measure

KPIs reveal what is happening across demand, capacity, revenue and customer mix.

02 — UNDERSTAND

Understand

Identify the economic and competitive factors explaining the observed performance.

03 — FORECAST

Forecast

Estimate future demand, booking behavior and expected commercial outcomes.

04 — OPTIMIZE

Optimize

Evaluate alternative pricing, inventory and capacity decisions under uncertainty.

05 — DECIDE

Decide

Select the commercial action with the strongest expected economic outcome.
